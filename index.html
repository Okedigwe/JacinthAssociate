About Jacinth & Associate

Jacinth & Associate is a Nigerian building design and construction company. We build homes and commercial structures that are safe, durable and finished to a high standard.

Our team handles design, structural work, finishing and site supervision under one roof, so you deal with one accountable contractor. We use quality materials, follow approved drawings and keep you informed at every stage, whether you are on site or overseas.

Frequently asked questions

Do you build outside one city?

Yes. We take on residential and commercial projects across Nigeria. Tell us your site location and we will confirm logistics and timelines.

How much does it cost to build a house in Nigeria?

Cost depends on the size, design, location, soil condition and finishing standard. After a site visit and a look at your drawings (or a design brief), we prepare a detailed Bill of Quantities so you know what each stage will cost.

Can you build in stages to match my budget?

Yes. Many clients build in phases: foundation, carcass (up to roofing), then finishing. We plan each phase so work can pause and resume without damage to the structure.

I live abroad. Can you manage my project?

Yes. We send regular photo and video progress updates and share a clear breakdown of spending at each stage, so you can follow your build from anywhere.

Do you help with building plans and approvals?

We prepare designs and 3D visuals and can guide you through the building plan approval process with the relevant state authority.
